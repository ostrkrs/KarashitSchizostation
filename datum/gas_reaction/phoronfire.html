Phoron combustion:

Combustion of oxygen and phoron (mostly treated as hydrocarbons). The reaction rate is dependent on the temperature of the gasmix. May produce either tritium or carbon dioxide and water vapor depending on the fuel/oxydizer ratio of the gasmix.
